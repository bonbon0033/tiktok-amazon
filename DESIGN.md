# TikTok Amazon Ranking — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Dark, urgent, slightly underground — TikTok-native energy. Near-black canvas with electric neon accents (lime + cyan). Tabular numerals for prices and rankings, oversized rank numbers as the primary visual hook. Bold sans-serif only, never decorative. Motion is snappy and confident, never gentle — every frame should feel like a stat dropping, not a brand reveal.

## Colors

- `#0a0a0a` — canvas (near-black, neutral)
- `#e8ff3a` — primary accent (electric lime — ranking numbers, prices, CTA)
- `#22e0ff` — secondary accent (cyan — star ratings, product borders)
- `#ff3464` — alert accent (saturated red — taglines, urgency text)
- `#f5f5f5` — body text
- `#888888` — muted labels
- `#1a1a1a` — secondary surface (card backgrounds when needed)

## Typography

- `Anton` — ranking numbers, hero headlines (very heavy display sans)
- `Inter` — body, product names, prices, descriptions (700–900 weights)

## What NOT to Do

- No soft gradients on the canvas (H.264 banding)
- No script fonts, no serif, no rounded "friendly" fonts
- No pastel colors — saturation stays high
- No drop shadows on text — rely on color contrast
- No animated emoji or kawaii illustrations
- No corporate brand-reveal pacing — every entrance should feel urgent
